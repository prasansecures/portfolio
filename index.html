the launchpad

the houses and the launchpad were never two programmes. they were one funnel: the strongest teams out of every house carried straight into three months of structured follow-through, because demo day is the halfway point.

intake

best of the housesno open call. the teams that shipped hardest in the residency got the seat.

3 months

structured follow-upweekly check-ins, intros, debriefs. the unglamorous part nobody runs.

outcome

products, not demosprojects harden into things people use. ARlink is the loudest example.

then

follow-on fundingthe round closes months after the applause, because someone stayed in it.
